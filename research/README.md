# Research

This directory contains original research projects developed under Project SENTRI.
Each subdirectory represents an individual research initiative exploring topics related to AI safety, AI security, and responsible AI. Research projects are developed independently and may include literature synthesis, technical analysis, experimental findings, and supporting resources.
Completed projects may later be refined and published through the Project SENTRI Publications repository.

---

## Scope

Research topics may include:
- AI Safety
- AI Security
- LLM Security
- AI Alignment
- AI Governance
- Model Evaluation
- AI Risk Assessment
- Agent Security
- Responsible AI

---

## Repository Structure
Each research project is maintained within its own directory to keep research organized, reproducible, and easy to expand over time.
Example:
research/
└── prompt-injection-taxonomy/
    ├── README.md
    ├── draft.md
    ├── references.md
    └── assets/

---

## Status
Research projects are considered living documents and may evolve as new findings, publications, and evidence become available.
